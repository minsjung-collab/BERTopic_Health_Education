# Data

For an exact from-scratch replication, place the archived May 24, 2026 analytical corpus here as `final_df.csv` and ensure that the column consumed by the modeling script is `Combined_Text` (or update `TEXT_COLUMN` to the exact archived column name).

The included PubMed retrieval/preprocessing script can regenerate the corpus from the documented ISSN/date queries. However, a later PubMed retrieval may not be byte-for-byte identical to the May 24, 2026 corpus because PubMed records can be corrected or re-indexed retrospectively.

The paper reports 4,234 records in the final analytical corpus.
